1. Introduction & Task Framing

Organic search traffic decays non-linearly across aging content libraries. Manual evaluation of thousands of URLs is unsustainable, leading to reactive updates only after significant traffic drops occur.

  • The Decision Supported: Construct a deterministic ranking queue to triage high-risk assets and prescribe precise editorial intervention before traffic dropoff.
  • Core Problem: Rule heuristics fail to capture non-linear interactions between CTR degradation, ranking position, and domain-wide age curves.

2. Data Contract & Public Sanitization

Trained and evaluated on the anonymized FlyRank Enterprise Warehouse dataset (dim_content & fact_search_performance).

  • Privacy Guardrails: Zero exposure of real client URLs, domains, or query strings. Identifiers are salted and cryptographically hashed.
  • Pre-Intervention Feature Windows: All features are computed over 90-day rolling windows strictly prior to target observation to prevent lookahead bias.
  • Tail Noise Exclusion: Sparse search queries with fewer than 5 lifetime impressions were excluded to prevent overfitting on long-tail artifacts.

3. Methodology & Leakage-Free Split

Standard random train/test splits cause extreme data leakage in search ranking tasks by scattering documents belonging to the same search intent across splits.

  • Grouped Split Policy: Enforced strict GroupShuffleSplit (60% Train, 20% Val, 20% Test) partitioned by domain/query entities.
  • Generalization Verification: The model evaluates completely unseen query clusters during testing, simulating true enterprise deployment.

5. Operational Action Playbook

Model probabilities are mapped into deterministic, human-reviewed action codes:

COMPREHENSIVE_REFRESH

Staleness Decay

Triggered when model risk ≥ 0.70 and document age > 180 days without updates. Requires complete factual and topical review.

TITLE_CTR_OPTIMIZE

CTR Underperformance

High-ranking assets (Position ≤ 15) with sub-2% CTR. Requires metadata, title tag, and schema markup enhancements.

CONSOLIDATE_OR_PRUNE

Tail Asset Pruning

Low impression assets (<100 impressions / 30d) aging past 250 days. Queued for topic clustering or canonicalization.

Strict No-Go Automation List:

No programmatic URL deletion, no unreviewed title tag publishing, and no automated bulk 301 redirection. All queued actions require human editorial review.

6. Research Limitations & Claim Boundaries

  • Directional Decision Support: Predictions quantify empirical risk correlation, not causal guarantees.
  • External Search Algorithm Shifts: Core algorithm updates and competitor backlink strategies are external to metadata features.
  • Cold-Start Variance: Higher prediction uncertainty on newly indexed pages with fewer than 10 impressions.
